## What is shown

The team introduces Trend Detective, asks whether Svalbard is getting warmer,
confirms AI-proposed settings, opens the NASA POWER investigation and asks a
follow-up about what cannot be concluded. Provider response waits are cut; the
video is not a latency benchmark or a claim that every question is answered.
