# Seasons in Trend Detective

This note was written by the Trend Detective project. It explains how the app treats seasons. It is the project's own description, not a NASA method.

## Which seasons

Besides all months, the app analyses four seasons named by their months: December to February, March to May, June to August and September to November. Seasons are named by their months because "winter" means different months in each hemisphere.

## Seasonal rates are descriptive

Each season's rate is a descriptive Sen's slope from the season's complete years. Only the all-months row of annual two-metre air temperature can be assessed with the trend test; every other row with a rate says that significance was not assessed. A season with fewer than ten complete years has no rate.

## Comparing seasons

Rates for different seasons can differ by chance alone, and no difference between seasons was tested. A season with a larger rate than another is not evidence that the seasons are changing differently.
