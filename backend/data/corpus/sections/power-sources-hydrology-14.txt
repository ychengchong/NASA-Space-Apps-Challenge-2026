POWER Hydrology Data Sources: Parameters
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

Beyond the three wetness-fraction parameters shared with MERRA-2, the SMAP pathway adds volumetric moisture and climatological percentiles:

Parameter | Long Name | Units | Notes

SFMC | Surface Soil Moisture (0–5 cm) | m³/m³ | Volumetric water content

RZMC | Root-Zone Soil Moisture | m³/m³ | 0–100 cm column

PRMC | Profile Soil Moisture | m³/m³ | Full model column

SFMC_PRCNTL | Surface Moisture Percentile | % | Relative to 2015–present

RZMC_PRCNTL | Root-Zone Moisture Percentile | % | Relative to 2015–present

Additionally, SMAP provides six soil-temperature layers, evaporation, and land-surface radiation fluxes.
