POWER Meteorological Data Sources: Precipitation
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

This section compares MERRA-2 precipitation to observations reported to NCEI. MERRA-2 precipitation over land incorporates an observationally corrected precipitation forcing (PRECTOTCORR vs. PRECTOT in ordering data), which is known to improve agreement with gauge-based records relative to purely model-generated precipitation. (Reichle, et al., 2017)
