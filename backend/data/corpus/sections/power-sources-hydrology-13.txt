POWER Hydrology Data Sources: SMAP Processing
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

- Source product: NASA produces the L4_SM global daily analysis on a 9 km Equal-Area Scalable Earth (EASE) grid.

- Regridding: The POWER pipeline uses the standard latitude–longitude grid at approximately 0.09° spacing. Reichle et al., 2017 SMAP L4_SM, 2026

- Temporal aggregation: POWER serves SMAP at the daily UTC temporal level. Monthly and annual aggregates are computed by averaging daily values.

- Storage: Processed fields are stored as Zarr arrays in the POWER datastore on AWS (s3://nasa-power-smap/smap/).
