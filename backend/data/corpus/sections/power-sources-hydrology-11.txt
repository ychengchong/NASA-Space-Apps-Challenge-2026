POWER Hydrology Data Sources: Possible Use Cases
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

- Reservoir-storage monitoring for hydroelectric power planning.

- Lake-level anomaly tracking for drought / flood early warning.

- River-stage time series for transboundary basins lacking in-situ gauges.

- Calibration / validation of land-surface and hydrologic models.

Validation

POWER has not independently validated GWM parameters. Please see FAQs, documents, and references at GWM for more information on accuracy and validation.
