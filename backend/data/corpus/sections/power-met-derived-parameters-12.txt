POWER Meteorological Derived Parameters: Growing Degree Days
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

Another application of temperature data is in the usage of Growing Degree Days (GDD). GDD, like HDD and CDD, are based upon the deviation of the daily averaged temperature, typically evaluated as (T_{max} + T_{min})/2, relative to a reference temperature, T_{base}. GDD are measures of the heat accumulation of a location which allow prediction of different growth stages of crops and emergence of insects.
