POWER Validation Data Sources: Temporal Aggregation
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

Hourly and daily AERONET in POWER are sourced differently, where daily averages are already available from the AERONET project, hourly must be aggregated from raw minute-based data. Daily AOD 550 and 840 are interpolated as described above and do not need temporal aggregation.

Raw AERONET data is aggregated to hourly values for POWER users in a similar method as how BSRN is handled. The minute-based data is averaged to 15-minute and require at least 1 valid value. The hourly values are averaged from 4 valid 15-minute values or else no value can be assigned. Daily values are a result of the average of at least 5 hourly values. Since POWER does not have sub-hourly core data, the minute-based and 15-minute averages are discarded and only the hourly values are kept.

Once it is curated and saved in POWER's storage solution, the data is ready for users to validate with it in the DAV or API.

Data Latency

While AERONET updates their data sets daily, POWER accesses and processes the complete data set on a monthly basis.
