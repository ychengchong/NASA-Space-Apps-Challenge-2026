POWER Meteorological Derived Parameters: Equation
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

\begin{align} T_{min} = max(T_{min},T_{base}) \tag {Minimum Daily Temperature adjustment} \end{align}

\begin{align} T_{i} = (T_{max} + T_{min})/2 \tag {Equation 1} \end{align}

\begin{align} GDD = max(T_{i}-T_{base},0) \tag {Equation 2} \end{align}

\begin{align} Where: \\ T_{i}: & \text{ The base temperature, dependent on plant/insect type. }\\ T_{base}: & \text{ The daily mean temperature from the 24 hourly components. }\\ T_{max}: & \text{ The daily maximum temperature from the 24 hourly components. }\\ T_{min}: & \text{ The daily minimum temperature from the 24 hourly components. } \end{align}
