POWER does not ingest GWM data from the main GWM portal. The GWM team provides a curated package of multi-resolution radar altimetry products specifically for Hydroelectric Power (HEP) reservoirs, updated regularly at a dedicated distribution point. This curated data set is a subset of the broader GWM product suite, filtered to reservoirs with identified HEP dams.
