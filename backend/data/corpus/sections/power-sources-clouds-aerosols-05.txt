AOD at 0.55 µm Validation

Validation of CERES SYN1deg AOD at 0.55 µm against AERONET surface observations.

Comparison of CERES SYN1deg AOD at 550 nm against AERONET Level 2.0 observations at Boulder, Colorado (BOU), 2001–2016. Left: Hexbin scatter plots of matched pairs. Right: SYN1deg − AERONET difference by calendar month.

Plot Notes

- Statistics include bias, root mean squared error (RMSE), Pearson's correlation coefficient, Mean Absolute Error and number of matched data pairs.

- Validation comparisons utilize quality-assured Level 2.0 AERONET data matched spatially and temporally to the 1° SYN1deg grid.
