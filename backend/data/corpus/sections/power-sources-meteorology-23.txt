POWER Meteorological Data Sources: Validation IMERG vs. MERRA-2
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

The POWER project compared accumulated precipitation from the NCEI GSOD observations to two precipitation sources: the Integrated Multi-satellitE Retrievals for GPM (IMERG) and MERRA-2. This comparison was done to evaluate the performance between the higher resolution IMERG against the MERRA-2 data product. Independent evaluations have found that satellite-gauge products such as IMERG can better capture spatial precipitation structure than coarser reanalyses, though relative skill varies by region, season, and accumulation period.

Two test cases were completed to conduct this comparison:

- All Events: pairs compared at the weekly and monthly level.

- Precipitation Only: pairs compared at the weekly and monthly level where precipitation was greater than zero.

The IMERG data is only available at the daily temporal in UTC time for POWER's services.
