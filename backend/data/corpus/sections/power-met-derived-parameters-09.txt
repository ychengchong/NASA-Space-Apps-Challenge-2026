POWER Meteorological Derived Parameters: Heating/Cooling Degree Days
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

An important application of historical temperature data is in the calculation of Heating Degree Days (HDD) and Cooling Degree Days (CDD). Degree-day indices are the integrated departure of daily mean temperature from a base temperature and are widely used to estimate building heating and cooling energy demand.
