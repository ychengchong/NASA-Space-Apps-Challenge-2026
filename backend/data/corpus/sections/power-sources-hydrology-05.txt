POWER Hydrology Data Sources: POWER's HEP Reservoir Focus
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

POWER currently serves GWM data for approximately 340 reservoirs that have been identified as having Hydroelectric Power (HEP) stations in their basins. GWM publishes per-reservoir altimetry time series for hydroelectric and other major reservoirs through its lake/reservoir index. The identification process involves two steps:

- Ground-track collocation — The GWM team examines satellite altimeter ground tracks in tools such as Google Earth and identifies which tracks cross an inland water body.

- Hydroelectric classification — Reservoir catalogs and databases are used to determine whether the water body hosts an HEP station. Only those reservoirs with confirmed HEP infrastructure are included in the curated POWER subset.
