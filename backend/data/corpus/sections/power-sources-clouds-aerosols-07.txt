Cloud Pressure | Cloud pressure from the TEMPO CLDO4 V04 Level 3 product | hPa

Note on Usage: Users should note that while Surface Ozone (O3_SFC) and Surface Pressure represent surface conditions, parameters like NO2_VCD and HCHO_VCD represent total quantities in the atmospheric column. While these column densities are strong indicators of localized surface emission patterns, they do not directly equate to surface-level mixing ratios (e.g., parts per billion) typically measured by ground sensors. For nitrogen dioxide, POWER distributes the tropospheric column, which excludes the stratospheric contribution and so isolates the portion of the atmosphere influenced by surface emissions.
