POWER Hydrology Data Sources: Parameters
Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

GWM provides (per feature) parameters such as:

- Water surface height anomaly (relative to a feature-specific reference).

- Absolute water surface elevation (where reference geoid is established).

- Time stamp of each observation (irregular per orbital revisit).

- Quality / validity flag.

Derived products may include lake-area, reservoir-storage, and river-discharge proxies; those are mission- and feature-dependent and documented per product on the GWM portal.
