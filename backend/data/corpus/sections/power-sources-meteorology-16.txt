Publisher: NASA Langley Research Center, POWER Project

Wind Direction at a given height in degrees is the meteorological convention. The meteorological convention gives the direction from which the wind originates, and wind direction is measured in degrees clockwise from due north. This convention (0°/360° = north, 90° = east) is the standard adopted by the WMO and NWS for reporting surface and upper-air winds.

def WD(data, Level): ''' Wind Direction - data: Xarray Dataset with the variables units defined (required) - Level: the elevation to compute the wind direction (required) ''' U_Values = Convert(data["U{}M".format(Level)]).To("m/s") V_Values = Convert(data["V{}M".format(Level)]).To("m/s") WD_Values = np.arctan2(-U_Values, -V_Values) * (180 / np.pi) WD_Values = np.where(WD_Values >= 0, WD_Values, WD_Values + 360) WD_Values = xr.DataArray(WD_Values, dims=('time', 'latitude', 'longitude',)) WD_Values.name = "WD{}M".format(Level) WD_Values.attrs['units'] = "Degrees" return WD_Values
